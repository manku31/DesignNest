# Generated panorama assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project. All three final PNGs are 1774 × 887 pixels (2:1) and live in `public/panoramas/`. The prompts requested full 360° × 180° equirectangular projections. These are illustrative AI concepts, not verified scans of the stock-photo rooms; geometry and the wrap seam may contain generation artifacts. The source outputs are preserved in the generation tool's asset folder, and unmodified copies are served locally by the app.

The viewer maps each image to a sphere with Pannellum. Play Tour animates the camera around that sphere; it does not generate a recorded video file. Replacing these images with measured, seamless 2:1 panoramas improves geometric accuracy without changing the viewer.

## living-room

Asset: `public/panoramas/living-room-panorama.png`

Final prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: true 360-degree spherical panorama texture for an interactive interior-design web app, NOT a normal wide-angle photo. Generate a single high-resolution image in exactly 2:1 aspect ratio, ideally 3072 by 1536 pixels, in full equirectangular (latitude-longitude) projection covering 360 degrees horizontally and 180 degrees vertically. Camera fixed at the center of the room at eye level, horizon at the vertical midpoint. All four sides of the same coherent room wrap across the image. Left and right edges must join seamlessly with the same architecture and lighting. The top edge is the ceiling zenith, the bottom edge is the floor nadir; include the correct polar distortion for a spherical map. Straight verticals around the equator, natural panoramic curvature above and below. Photorealistic architectural photography, premium warm minimalism, warm beige and taupe with walnut, soft amber ambient lighting, tactile materials, believable room proportions. No people, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no black borders, no separate frames, no collage. Do not render a UI or a picture of a panorama viewer.
Scene: a luxurious modern living room. At the central viewing direction, a low cream bouclé sectional sofa against softly illuminated warm plaster, a round walnut coffee table on a large textured beige rug, a wide sculptural beige dome pendant overhead. Around the full room: sheer-curtained tall windows with late-afternoon light, an olive tree, a dark walnut media console, quiet abstract artwork, a reading armchair and lamp, and a doorway into a hallway. Oak floor continuous across the floor hemisphere; smooth warm plaster ceiling. Comfortable, intimate, refined, uncluttered.
